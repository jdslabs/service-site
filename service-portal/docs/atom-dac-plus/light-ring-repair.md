# Light Ring Repair

Repair the Atom DAC+ light ring.

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Time:** 5 minutes  
**Tools:** Torx screwdriver

---

<div class="repair-step">
  <div class="repair-step-media"></div>
  <div class="repair-step-content">
    <div class="repair-step-header">
      <span class="repair-step-number">1</span>
      <h3 class="repair-step-title">Disconnect Power</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Unplug the power cable from your Atom DAC+</li>
      <li>Disconnect any audio cables (USB, TOSLINK, RCA)</li>
    </ul>
    <div class="repair-warning">
      Always disconnect power before opening the enclosure to avoid electrical shock or damage.
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="repair-step">
  <div class="repair-step-media">
    <img src="/img/atom-dac-plus/light-ring-1.JPG" alt="Four Torx screws on the bottom of the Atom DAC+" />
  </div>
  <div class="repair-step-content">
    <div class="repair-step-header">
      <span class="repair-step-number">2</span>
      <h3 class="repair-step-title">Remove the Feet and Screws</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Remove the adhesive feet and unscrew all four Torx screws on the bottom of the device</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="repair-step">
  <div class="repair-step-media">
    <img src="/img/atom-dac-plus/light-ring-2.JPG" alt="Top panel removed from the Atom DAC+" />
  </div>
  <div class="repair-step-content">
    <div class="repair-step-header">
      <span class="repair-step-number">3</span>
      <h3 class="repair-step-title">Remove the Top Panel</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Remove the top panel and flip the device back upright</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="repair-step">
  <div class="repair-step-media">
    <img src="/img/atom-dac-plus/light-ring-3.JPG" alt="Notch in the outer white portion of the light ring" />
  </div>
  <div class="repair-step-content">
    <div class="repair-step-header">
      <span class="repair-step-number">4</span>
      <h3 class="repair-step-title">Locate the Light Ring Notch</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Locate the notch in the outer white portion of the light ring, this will align with a notch in the bottom interior of the case</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="repair-step">
  <div class="repair-step-media">
    <img src="/img/atom-dac-plus/light-ring-4.JPG" alt="Light ring seated into the case notch" />
  </div>
  <div class="repair-step-content">
    <div class="repair-step-header">
      <span class="repair-step-number">5</span>
      <h3 class="repair-step-title">Seat the Light Ring</h3>
    </div>
    <ul>
      <li>Ensuring the light ring has fully seated into the notch, press the ring against the front wall of the case with the entirety of the adhesive contacting the surface</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
